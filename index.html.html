🔍 What Accenture SFDC Training Looks Like (Real Intel)

📌
Duration: 4–5 weeks stream training. You'll work on a mini case study + a 3-hour hands-on coding exam + a 40-question MCQ test.
📌
Case Study: 5-day team project (build a Salesforce app). External examiner evaluates your contribution + asks individual questions.
📌
Hands-On Exam: 3-hour session. You get a problem statement — build it in Salesforce. Admin + Apex + Trigger tasks are most common.
📌
MCQ Test: ~40 questions. Moderate to tough. Concept-based — not direct copy from material.
📌
Passing Score: 60% first attempt. 65% if resitting. Don't rely on resit!
📌
Key Tip: Understand what YOU built in the case study. The examiner grills you individually on your own code.
